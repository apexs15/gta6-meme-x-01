# gta6-meme-x-01

Jason & Lucia hired a ghost as their getaway driver in Vice City — and the ghost never gets caught, he gets paid. A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's launch-week bounty: the make.fun ghost, white with sparkle eyes and its magenta star coin, drives the getaway car through neon Leonida while Jason & Lucia ride in the back.

The meme is `meme.png`, a 1024x1024 PNG rendered locally from HTML and SVG, with the ghost drawn from make.fun's press-kit mark.
